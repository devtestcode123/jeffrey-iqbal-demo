About Jeffrey Iqbal

USA born and bred, Jeffrey Iqbal has been entertaining audiences globally. He has performed with A.R. Rahman, Shreya Ghoshal, Shankar Mahadevan, and Atif Aslam.

Legacy

Jeffrey brings Bollywood, pop and world music to high-end events, weddings and concerts with a modern, cinematic stage experience.

Mission

Unity Thru Music — a global show built around connection, rhythm and the shared power of song.
