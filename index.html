Nº 02 — The artist

Bridging east & west.

USA born and bred, Jeffrey Iqbal made history as the first U.S.-born singer to sing playback for a Bollywood film (Dehraadun Diary). He has shared the stage with A.R. Rahman, Shreya Ghoshal, Shankar Mahadevan and Atif Aslam, and appeared on Sa Re Ga Ma Pa, India’s Raw Star and Jammin.
