ABOUT
The Artist

Bridging The
East & West

USA born and bred, Jeffrey Iqbal has been entertaining audiences and music royalty globally. Making history as the first U.S.-born singer to perform as a playback singer for a Bollywood film (Dehraadun Diary), his voice authentically brings together music from the East and West.

From classic to new Bollywood and new-aged English pop chartbusters, Jeffrey has shared the stage with legendary artists including A.R. Rahman, Shreya Ghoshal, Shankar Mahadevan, and Atif Aslam.

Amassing millions of views and appearing on major TV shows like Sa Re Ga Ma Pa, India’s Raw Star, and Jammin, he takes audiences on a beautiful journey—from romantic valleys to energetic highs.
